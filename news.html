Official announcements, calls for collaboration, and upcoming events. The center has been officially operating since August 1, 2026; event registration and the first project call will follow here.

AI services

AI platforms & teams already in service

Campus AI platforms and the student technical R&D team are now available to the NTPU community: NTPU AI, the university AI platform (AI conversation, web search, campus search, file analysis); NTPU AI Assistant, the campus question-answering assistant (Physical Education Office, Center for General Education, Language Center); and the NTPU AI Aquila Project technical R&D team (technology development, project implementation support, education & training). 2026-08-01
Announcement

AI4X Research Center officially began operations on August 1, 2026

The Advanced Research Center for Cross-Disciplinary AI (AI4X Research Center), a university-level research center of National Taipei University, has been officially operational since August 1, 2026. The center connects research strengths in law, business, public affairs, humanities and social sciences, sustainability, and information technology. Its governance structure is in place and the first-wave initiatives are under way; the remaining research, resource, and collaboration mechanisms will be launched in phases. Center Director: Prof. Min-Yuh Day (Professor & Director, Graduate Institute of Information Management, NTPU). Administrative contact: Ssu-Yin Chen, Administrative Specialist (ssuyin41@gm.ntpu.edu.tw, ext. 66894).

2026-07-18
Mission

Center mission and first-wave initiatives published

The center's founding mission (cross-disciplinary research, shared resources, industry-academia collaboration, and responsible AI) and its first-wave initiatives are now published. Initial work includes cross-college research matchmaking, campus AI capacity mapping, research data and computing governance, seed research projects, faculty workshops, and a single window for external collaboration. Each item is labeled with its current status and expected timing.

2026-07-18
Survey

Cross-domain collaboration and research needs survey

The center invites NTPU faculty and research teams to share their cross-domain collaboration interests and research needs. Responses inform the first wave of research matchmaking and the design of seed project calls. Please email ssuyin41@gm.ntpu.edu.tw with your name, unit, collaboration type, and a brief summary of your needs; see the contact page for details.

More announcements, including the first research group formations, the seed project call, and the center's first academic event, will be published here as they are confirmed.
